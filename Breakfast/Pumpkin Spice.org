* Pumpkin Spice
** Ingredients:
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- ¼ teaspoon ground allspice
- ¼ to ¾ teaspoon ground cloves, to taste
** Method:
1. Place the cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, allspice and cloves in a sieve set over a small bowl; sift to make sure you don’t have any clumps of spice.
2. Mix well, then transfer to an airtight container.
3. Store in a dark, cool space.
4. It will keep for as long as the spices stay fresh.
** Yield
2 tablespoons ground spice blend
