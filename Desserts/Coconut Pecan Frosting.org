* Coconut Pecan Frosting
** Ingredients:
- ¾ cup sugar
- ¼ cup butter
- 1 (5-ounce) can (⅔ cup) evaporated milk
- 1 large egg, slightly beaten
- 1 ¼ cups sweetened flaked coconut
- ½ cup chopped pecans
** Method:
1. Combine sugar, butter, evaporated milk and egg in 2-quart saucepan.
2. Cook, stirring constantly, over medium heat 6-8 minutes or until thickened and bubbly.
3. Stir in coconut and pecans.
4. Cool about 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until spreadable.
