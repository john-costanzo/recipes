* Apple or Peach Crisp
** Ingredients:
- 4 cups sliced tart apples or peaches (about 4 medium)
- ⅔ cup packed brown sugar
- ½ cup oats
- ½ cup flour
- ¾ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ¾ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- ⅓ cup butter, softened
** Method:
1. Heat oven to 375°.
2. Arrange fruit in a greased square pan.
3. Mix remaining ingredients.
4. Sprinkle over fruit.
5. Bake until tipping is golden brown and fruit is tender.
#+begin_tip
Serve warm and, if desired, with cream or ice cream.
#+end_tip
