* Frozen Food Processor Pesto
** Ingredients:
- 2 cups firmly packed basil leaves
- ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons pine nuts
- 2 cloves peeled garlic
- ½ cup Parmesan cheese
** Method:
1. Blend basil, pine nuts and garlic in a food processor until finely minced,
2. With motor running, slowly dribble in the oil and process until the mixture is smooth.
3. Add the cheese and process very briefly, just long enough to combine.
*** Storing
1. Ladle mixture into ice cube trays.
2. Top off with a little olive oil.
3. Cover with plastic wrap and freeze.
4. When solid, empty trays into plastic bags and store in freezer.
5. When ready to prepare pasta, gently thaw the desired amount of pesto (4 cubes per ½ pound of pasta).
6. When at room temperature, add 1 tablespoons Parmesan cheese to each cube.
*** Using
1. Boil water for pasta.
2. Add a couple of teaspoons of the pasta’s boiling water and stir until smooth.
3. Toss pasta in the sauce and serve immediately.
   #+begin_note
         Yield: 12 tablespoons (cubes) or enough for 1- 1½ pounds of pasta.
   #+end_note

   #+begin_warning
          Never, never, never heat or reheat pesto sauce. It will become bitter, so no leftovers!!!
   #+end_warning
