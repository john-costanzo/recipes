* Pizza (a la Vito Iacopelli)
** Ingredients:
*** Poolish
- 300 ml room temperature water
- 300 g flour (00 or all purpose)
- 5 g dry yeast
- 5 g honey
*** Pizza Dough
- 700 ml of water
- 40 g sea salt
- 1,250 g of flour
** Method:
*** Poolish
1. Start 25 hours before making pizza.
2. Combine water, yeast and honey in plastic container.
3. Add flour and mix until smooth with spoon.
4. Cover and leave for one hour at room temperature and then place in the refrigerator overnight.
*** Pizza
**** Make dough balls
1. Add water to larger bowl.
2. Then add the starter dough.
3. Mix with spoon.
4. Add half of the flour and mix.
5. Add salt and combine.
6. Add remaining flour.
7. Mix together and knead until smooth. (The dough will be sticky.)
8. Cover dough and let rest for 15 minutes.
9. Next, cover hands in olive oil.
10. Lift dough turning it under until it forms a big ball until smooth.
11. Add olive oil to large bowl.
12. Place ball in bowl and let sit for 1 hour.
13. Cover hands in olive oil.
14. Work dough into oval ball, then cut into three equal sections.
15. Turn each section into a ball (225 g) by wrapping edges under each other until you have a perfect ball.
16. Flour a proofing box and place pizza dough balls about 3 fingers apart.
17. Add little bit of oil on top of we each dough ball in case it rises and touches the lids.
18. Rest for two hours at room temperature.
**** Cook pizza
1. After one hour, put stone in oven and turn on to oven's maximum temperature.
2. Once dough has sat gently coat each side of ball in flour.
  #+begin_note
  Keep top side up at all times.
  #+end_note
3. Gently press ball working from middle to outside forming a circle.
4. Turn over and repeat forming a circle, pressing and pulling the dough to about 8 to 10 inches.
5. Add sauce and place pizza in oven.
6. Cook until golden, then remove pizza from oven.
7. Add cheese, other toppings and more olive oil.
8. Return to the oven and cook until the cheese has melted.
